TURNSTILE

An agent walks to the turnstile and time turns round. Going forward, a trajectory moves him through the keyframes of a walk cycle and he leaves footprints and breaks a vase. Coming back, the head runs back along the chain and the inverted functions undo it all: the footprints lift, the vase puts itself back together.
